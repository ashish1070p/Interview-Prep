The live transcript can't run in this copy. Chrome and Edge only allow speech-to-text on pages opened from a web address, and this page is open as a file from your computer. The camera, panel and recording still work.

To fix it, open the room from a link. Upload this file to GitHub Pages or any web host, or run a local server: open a terminal in this file's folder, run python -m http.server 8000, then go to http://localhost:8000/.

Your CV, video and transcript stay in this browser; nothing is uploaded. Chrome and Edge send your voice to their speech service to make the live transcript, and reading a PDF loads the free pdf.js library from a public CDN.

  • Wear headphones, so the panel's voice stays out of your recording.
  • Look at the camera lens when you answer, not at your own face.
  • Press Space when you finish an answer. R repeats the question.
If something doesn't work

No camera picture. Allow camera and microphone from the icon in the address bar. Close Teams or Zoom if they are using the camera, then press Retry camera.

No live transcript. Use Chrome or Edge, and open the room from a web address (https or localhost), not as a file. Brave, Safari and Firefox don't support it. Press Test transcript to see the exact problem.

Transcript says the speech service can't be reached. It needs internet, and some office or campus networks block it. Try a phone hotspot.

My PDF won't read. Scanned PDFs have no text in them. Export your CV as a text-based PDF or Word file, or paste the text. Reading a PDF also needs an internet connection.

No panel voice. Check your volume and choose a voice on the right. Edge has the most natural voices.
